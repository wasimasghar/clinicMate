# Thyroid Problems

## What is the thyroid
The thyroid is a small butterfly-shaped gland in the front of the neck. It makes hormones that control the body's energy, weight, heart rate and temperature. Thyroid problems are more common in women.

## Underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism)
The thyroid makes too little hormone. Symptoms:
- Tiredness and feeling slow.
- Weight gain.
- Feeling cold.
- Dry skin and hair fall.
- Constipation.
- Low mood.
- Heavy or irregular periods.

## Overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism)
The thyroid makes too much hormone. Symptoms:
- Weight loss despite good appetite.
- Fast heartbeat or palpitations.
- Feeling hot and sweating.
- Shaking hands, anxiety, poor sleep.
- Loose motions.
- Bulging eyes in some people.

## Goitre (thyroid swelling)
A swelling in the front of the neck. In parts of Pakistan, especially northern areas, goitre has been linked to lack of iodine in food. Using iodised salt helps prevent it.

## Tests
A blood test (TSH, and sometimes T3 and T4) shows how the thyroid is working. An ultrasound may be done for swelling or lumps.

## Treatment and care
Thyroid problems are usually well controlled with treatment prescribed by a doctor and regular blood tests. Women planning pregnancy or who are pregnant should get thyroid checked.

## When to see a doctor
See an Endocrinologist or General Physician for the symptoms above, or any swelling or lump in the neck. Go to emergency for a very fast heartbeat with fever and confusion.

## Common questions

- Thyroid ka test kaun sa hai? TSH blood test.
- Gale mein soojan hai? Goitre ho sakta hai, doctor ko dikhayein.
- Iodine wala namak kyun zaroori hai? Ye goitre se bachata hai.
