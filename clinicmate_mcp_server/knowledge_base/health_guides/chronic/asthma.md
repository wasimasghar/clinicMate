# Asthma

## What is asthma
Asthma is a long-term condition in which the airways in the lungs become swollen and narrow, making breathing difficult. It often starts in childhood and often runs in families with allergies. It cannot be cured but can be well controlled.

## Symptoms
- Wheezing (a whistling sound when breathing).
- Shortness of breath.
- Chest tightness.
- Cough, especially at night or early morning.
Symptoms come and go and can suddenly get worse (an asthma attack).

## Common triggers
- Smog and air pollution, very high in Lahore and Punjab in winter.
- Dust, dust mites, mould and pet dander.
- Pollen, especially paper mulberry pollen in Islamabad in spring.
- Cigarette, shisha and stove smoke.
- Strong perfume, sprays and incense.
- Cold air, colds and flu.
- Exercise in some people.

## Treatment
A Pulmonologist or doctor decides treatment. Asthma is usually treated with inhalers: one type to relieve symptoms quickly and another to prevent attacks when used regularly. Learning the correct inhaler technique is very important. A spacer helps children and elderly people.

## Living with asthma
- Know and avoid your triggers. Check AQI and wear a mask on high smog days.
- Keep the house free of smoke and dust.
- Use preventer treatment regularly if prescribed, even when you feel well.
- Get the yearly flu vaccine if your doctor advises.
- Have a written asthma action plan from your doctor.

## Emergency signs
Call 1122 or go to emergency at once if breathing is very difficult, you cannot speak a full sentence, lips or fingertips turn blue or grey, the reliever inhaler does not help, or a child is struggling to breathe or very drowsy.

## Common questions
- Saans phoolna aur seeti ki awaaz? Ye asthma ho sakta hai, Pulmonologist ko dikhayein.
- Smog mein asthma barh jata hai? Haan, mask pehnein aur AQI dekh kar bahar niklein.
- Inhaler ki aadat ho jati hai? Nahi, inhaler asthma ka mehfooz aur aam ilaaj hai.
- Bolne mein mushkil aur honth neelay? Foran 1122 par call karein.
