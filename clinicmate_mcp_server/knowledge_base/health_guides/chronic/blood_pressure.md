# High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)

## What is high blood pressure
Blood pressure is the force of blood against the walls of the arteries. High blood pressure means this force stays too high. A reading of 140/90 or above on repeated checks is usually called high blood pressure. It is often called a "silent killer" because most people feel nothing until damage is done.

## Causes and risk factors
- Too much salt, including pickles (achar), papad, salty snacks and processed food.
- Being overweight and not exercising.
- Smoking, naswar and tobacco.
- Stress and poor sleep.
- Family history and older age.
- Diabetes and kidney disease.

## Symptoms
Usually none. Some people get headache, dizziness, blurred vision or nosebleeds when pressure is very high. The only way to know is to check it.

## Checking blood pressure
- Adults should check at least once a year, and more often if they have diabetes, are overweight or have a family history.
- Sit quietly for 5 minutes before checking, feet flat, arm supported at heart level.
- A home digital BP machine helps track readings; note them down for your doctor.

## Complications
Uncontrolled high blood pressure can cause heart attack, stroke, heart failure, kidney damage and vision loss.

## Living with high blood pressure
- Reduce salt; avoid adding extra salt at the table.
- Eat more fruit, vegetables and lentils; less ghee and fried food.
- Walk at least 30 minutes most days and keep a healthy weight.
- Stop smoking and tobacco.
- Take treatment exactly as advised and do not stop it when readings become normal.

## Emergency signs
Call 1122 at once for chest pain, severe headache with confusion, difficulty speaking, weakness or numbness of the face, arm or leg, or sudden vision loss. Remember stroke signs: face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty, time to call 1122.

## Common questions
- BP kitna hona chahiye? Aam taur par 120/80 ke aas paas, 140/90 ya zyada high hai.
- High BP ki alamat? Aksar koi nahi, is liye check karte rehna zaroori hai.
- Namak kam karne se faida hai? Haan, BP kam hota hai.
- BP ke liye kaun sa doctor? Cardiologist ya General Physician.
