# Food Allergy

## What is food allergy
Food allergy is when the body's defence system reacts to a food as if it were harmful. Even a small amount of the food can cause a reaction, usually within minutes to 2 hours of eating it.

## Common food allergens
- Cow's milk (common in babies and young children).
- Eggs.
- Peanuts and tree nuts (almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios).
- Fish and shellfish such as prawns.
- Wheat.
- Soy and sesame.
Many children grow out of milk and egg allergy; peanut, nut and seafood allergy often stays for life.

## Symptoms
- Itching or tingling in the mouth, lips or throat.
- Hives, rash or redness of the skin.
- Swelling of the lips, face or eyes.
- Stomach pain, vomiting or diarrhoea.
- Sneezing, runny nose, cough or wheezing.

## Food allergy or food intolerance
Food intolerance (for example lactose intolerance, when milk causes gas, bloating and loose motion) is not an allergy. It affects digestion, is not dangerous, and depends on the amount eaten. Food allergy involves the body's defence system and can be life-threatening.

## Living with food allergy
- Avoid the food completely and read food labels carefully.
- Tell restaurants, cooks, school and relatives about the allergy.
- Be careful with sweets, bakery items, biscuits and sauces, which may contain nuts, milk or eggs.
- People with severe allergy should follow an emergency plan from their doctor.

## Tests
An Allergist can confirm food allergy with a skin prick test, blood test, and sometimes a supervised food test in the clinic. Do not test a suspected food at home.

## When to see a doctor
- Any reaction after eating a food, even if mild.
- A baby with rash, vomiting, poor weight gain or blood in stool after milk feeds.
An Allergist or Pediatrician (for children) can help.

## Emergency signs
Call 1122 or go to emergency at once for difficulty breathing, wheezing, swelling of the tongue or throat, hoarse voice, feeling faint, pale or floppy child, or collapse after eating. This is anaphylaxis and needs immediate treatment.

## Common questions
- Moong phali khane se honth soojh gaye? Ye food allergy ho sakti hai, Allergist ko dikhayein.
- Doodh peene se pet kharab hota hai? Ye aksar lactose intolerance hoti hai, allergy nahi.
- Bachche ko anday se allergy hai, kya theek hogi? Aksar bachche bade ho kar theek ho jate hain, doctor se test karwayein.
- Khane ke baad saans phoolne lagi? Foran 1122 par call karein.
