# Vertigo and Dizziness

## What is vertigo
Vertigo is a feeling that you or the room around you is spinning, even when you are still. It is different from feeling light-headed or weak. Balance is controlled by the inner ear, the eyes and the brain, so problems in any of these can cause it.

## Common causes
- BPPV (benign paroxysmal positional vertigo): tiny crystals move in the inner ear. Short spinning for seconds when turning in bed, lying down or looking up. Very common and treatable.
- Inner ear infection or inflammation (vestibular neuritis or labyrinthitis), often after a cold or flu.
- Meniere's disease: attacks of spinning with ringing in the ear, a full feeling and hearing loss.
- Migraine.
- Less commonly, problems in the brain such as a stroke.

## Symptoms
- Spinning or swaying feeling.
- Nausea or vomiting.
- Loss of balance, difficulty walking straight.
- Sometimes ringing in the ear or hearing loss.

## What helps
- Sit or lie down as soon as the spinning starts, to avoid falling.
- Get up slowly from bed and turn the head slowly.
- Keep the room lit at night and remove things you could trip over.
- Avoid driving or climbing heights during attacks.
- Special head movement exercises taught by an ENT Specialist or physiotherapist help BPPV.

## When to see a doctor
- Vertigo that keeps coming back or lasts more than a few days.
- Vertigo with hearing loss or ringing in the ear.
- Falls because of dizziness, especially in elderly people.
An ENT Specialist checks the inner ear. A Neurologist may be needed if a brain cause is suspected.

## Emergency signs
Call 1122 or go to emergency at once if vertigo comes with sudden severe headache, double vision, slurred speech, weakness or numbness of the face, arm or leg, difficulty walking, or fainting. These can be signs of a stroke.

## Common questions
- Sar ghoomta hai aur kamra ghoomta hua lagta hai? Isay vertigo kehte hain, ENT Specialist ko dikhayein.
- Bistar par karwat lene se chakkar aate hain? Ye aksar BPPV hota hai, jo exercises se theek ho jata hai.
- Chakkar ke saath zabaan ladkhadaye ya hath kamzor ho? Foran 1122 par call karein.
