# Tonsillitis

## What is tonsillitis
Tonsils are two small glands at the back of the throat. Tonsillitis is infection and swelling of the tonsils, usually caused by a virus and sometimes by bacteria. It is common in children and teenagers but can happen at any age.

## Symptoms
- Sore throat and pain when swallowing.
- Red, swollen tonsils, sometimes with white or yellow spots.
- Fever and body aches.
- Swollen glands in the neck.
- Bad breath, hoarse voice, ear pain.
- In children: not eating, drooling, stomach pain or vomiting.

## Home care
- Rest and drink plenty of warm fluids and water.
- Gargle with warm salt water (adults and older children).
- Eat soft food and avoid very spicy food.
- Avoid cold drinks if they make the pain worse, and avoid smoke.
Most tonsillitis gets better within a week.

## When to see a doctor
- Sore throat lasting more than a week, or high fever.
- White patches with high fever, as some bacterial infections need treatment.
- Tonsillitis many times a year, or loud snoring and pauses in breathing at night in a child.
An ENT Specialist decides if removal of the tonsils (tonsillectomy) is needed.

## Emergency signs
Go to emergency at once for difficulty breathing, not being able to swallow saliva, drooling, not being able to open the mouth, swelling on one side of the throat, or a muffled voice.

## Common questions
- Gala kharab hai aur nigalne mein dard hai? Ye tonsils ka infection ho sakta hai.
- Tonsils nikalwane padte hain? Sirf jab saal mein bohat baar infection ho, ENT doctor tay karta hai.
- Namak wale pani se gararay faida dete hain? Haan, dard mein aram dete hain.
- Saans lene mein mushkil ho? Foran emergency jayein.
