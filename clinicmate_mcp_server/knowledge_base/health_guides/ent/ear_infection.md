# Ear Infection

## What is an ear infection
An ear infection is an infection of the outer ear canal (otitis externa, often called swimmer's ear) or of the middle ear behind the ear drum (otitis media). Middle ear infections are very common in young children, often after a cold or flu.

## Causes
- Cold, flu or throat infection spreading to the middle ear.
- Water staying in the ear after bathing or swimming.
- Scratching the ear canal with cotton buds, pins or fingers.
- Smoke exposure at home, which increases infections in children.
- Bottle feeding a baby while lying flat.

## Symptoms
- Ear pain, which may be worse when lying down.
- Fever, especially in children.
- Discharge of pus or fluid from the ear.
- Itching or redness in the ear canal.
- Reduced hearing or a blocked feeling in the ear.
- In babies: pulling or rubbing the ear, crying, poor sleep and poor feeding.

## Home care and prevention
- Keep the ear dry. Do not put oil, water, garlic or other home remedies in the ear.
- Do not use cotton buds or any object inside the ear.
- Keep children away from cigarette and stove smoke.
- Feed babies in an upright position.
- Keep vaccinations up to date.
Many mild middle ear infections settle in a few days, but a doctor should decide if treatment is needed.

## When to see a doctor
- Ear pain lasting more than 2 to 3 days, or high fever.
- Any discharge from the ear, or discharge that keeps coming back.
- Hearing loss after an ear infection.
- Repeated ear infections in a child.
An ENT Specialist or Pediatrician can check the ear drum and advise treatment.

## Emergency signs
Go to emergency at once if there is swelling, redness or pain behind the ear, severe headache, stiff neck, confusion, or weakness of the face. These can mean the infection has spread.

## Common questions
- Kaan mein dard aur bukhar hai? Ye ear infection ho sakta hai, doctor ko dikhayein.
- Kaan se pani ya peep beh rahi hai? ENT Specialist ko zaroor dikhayein.
- Kaan mein tel dalna theek hai? Nahi, kuch bhi na daalein.
- Bachche ko baar baar kaan ka infection hota hai? ENT ya bachon ke doctor se check karwayein.
