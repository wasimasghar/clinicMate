# Typhoid Fever

## What is typhoid
Typhoid is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi. It spreads through food and water contaminated with the stool of an infected person. It is common in Pakistan, and since 2016 a drug-resistant type (XDR typhoid) has spread, especially in Sindh, which makes correct treatment by a doctor very important.

## Symptoms
Symptoms usually start 1 to 3 weeks after infection and build up slowly:
- Fever that rises day by day and lasts many days.
- Weakness and tiredness.
- Headache and body aches.
- Stomach pain, loss of appetite.
- Constipation or diarrhoea.
- Sometimes rose-coloured spots on the chest or stomach.

## Tests
A blood culture is the best test. A doctor decides which tests are needed. The common Widal test is often unreliable on its own.

## Treatment and care
- Typhoid needs treatment prescribed by a doctor. Do not take leftover or self-chosen medicines, as wrong or incomplete treatment increases drug resistance.
- Complete the full course even if you feel better.
- Rest, drink plenty of clean fluids and eat soft, light food.

## Complications
Without proper treatment, typhoid can cause bleeding or a hole in the intestine, which is life-threatening. Go to emergency at once for severe stomach pain, vomiting blood, black stool, or confusion.

## Prevention
- Drink boiled or properly filtered water.
- Wash hands with soap after using the toilet and before eating or cooking.
- Avoid raw salads, cut fruit and street food from unclean places.
- Eat freshly cooked hot food.
- Typhoid vaccine (typhoid conjugate vaccine) is part of Pakistan's routine child immunisation. Adults can also ask a doctor about it.

## When to see a doctor
See a General Physician for fever lasting more than 3 days, especially with weakness, stomach pain or a change in bowel habits.

## Common questions
- Typhoid kaise hota hai? Ganday pani aur khane se.
- Typhoid ki alamat? Roz barhta hua bukhar, kamzori, pet dard aur bhook na lagna.
- XDR typhoid kya hai? Typhoid ki aisi qisam jis par aam dawaiyan asar nahi kartin, is liye doctor se ilaaj zaroori hai.
- Typhoid ka teeka hai? Haan, typhoid conjugate vaccine.
