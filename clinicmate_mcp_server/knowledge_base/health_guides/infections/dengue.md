# Dengue Fever

## What is dengue
Dengue is a viral infection spread by the bite of the Aedes mosquito, which bites mostly during the day, especially early morning and evening. In Pakistan, dengue cases rise after the monsoon, usually from August to November, in cities like Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad and Karachi.

## Symptoms
Symptoms usually start 4 to 10 days after the bite:
- Sudden high fever.
- Severe headache and pain behind the eyes.
- Severe muscle and joint pain (called "breakbone fever").
- Nausea and vomiting.
- Skin rash.
- Tiredness and loss of appetite.
Most people get better in about a week.

## Warning signs of severe dengue
Danger often starts around the time the fever goes down (24 to 48 hours after). Go to hospital at once if there is:
- Severe stomach pain.
- Repeated vomiting.
- Bleeding from gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, or red spots under the skin.
- Fast breathing.
- Extreme tiredness, restlessness or confusion.
- Very little urine, cold hands and feet.

## Tests
A blood test (CBC to check platelets, and dengue NS1 or antibody test) confirms dengue. A doctor may repeat the platelet count daily.

## Care at home (mild dengue)
- Rest and drink plenty of fluids: water, ORS, soups and fresh juices.
- Do not take any painkiller without a doctor's advice; some common painkillers increase bleeding risk in dengue.
- Do not use injections or drips without a doctor.
- Watch for warning signs, especially when the fever drops.

## Prevention
- Remove standing water: flower pots, coolers, tyres, buckets, water tanks and roof gutters. The mosquito breeds in clean still water.
- Cover water storage containers.
- Use mosquito repellent, especially during the day.
- Wear long sleeves and full trousers.
- Use window nets and mosquito nets, also for daytime naps.

## When to see a doctor
See a General Physician for any high fever with body pain during the dengue season, and get a blood test. Children, pregnant women, elderly people and people with diabetes or heart disease should see a doctor early.

## Common questions

- Dengue ka machhar kab kaat'ta hai? Zyada tar din mein, subah aur shaam.
- Bukhar utarne ke baad bhi khatra hai? Haan, bukhar utarte waqt warning signs ka khayal rakhein.
