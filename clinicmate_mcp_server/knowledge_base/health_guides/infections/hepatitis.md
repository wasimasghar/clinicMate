# Hepatitis B and C

## What is hepatitis
Hepatitis means swelling of the liver. Hepatitis B and C are viral infections that can stay in the body for years (chronic) and slowly damage the liver. Pakistan has one of the highest numbers of hepatitis C patients in the world, and many people do not know they are infected. Hepatitis A and E are different: they spread through dirty food and water and usually get better on their own.

## How hepatitis B and C spread
- Reused or unsterilised syringes and drips.
- Blood transfusion that is not properly screened.
- Unsterile dental, surgical or tattoo equipment.
- Shared razors at barber shops, shared toothbrushes or nail cutters.
- Hepatitis B also spreads through unprotected sex and from mother to baby at birth.
It does not spread through hugging, sharing food or shaking hands.

## Symptoms
Many people have no symptoms for years. When present:
- Tiredness and weakness.
- Loss of appetite, nausea.
- Yellow eyes and skin (jaundice), dark urine.
- Pain in the upper right side of the stomach.
Late signs of liver damage: swelling of the stomach or legs, vomiting blood, confusion.

## Tests
Simple blood tests (HBsAg for hepatitis B, anti-HCV and PCR for hepatitis C) show infection. Everyone in Pakistan should get tested at least once, especially people who had injections, drips, surgery, dental work or blood transfusion in the past.

## Treatment
- Hepatitis C is curable in most people with a few months of treatment prescribed by a doctor.
- Hepatitis B usually cannot be cured, but treatment can control it and protect the liver.
- Regular follow-up is needed to check the liver and screen for liver cancer.

## Complications
Untreated chronic hepatitis can lead to cirrhosis (scarring of the liver), liver failure and liver cancer.

## Prevention
- Insist on a new, sealed syringe for every injection. Avoid unnecessary injections and drips.
- Use your own razor; ask the barber to use a new blade.
- Get blood only from screened blood banks.
- Hepatitis B vaccine is part of Pakistan's routine child immunisation. Adults who are not vaccinated should ask a doctor.
- Babies of mothers with hepatitis B need the vaccine at birth.

## Common questions
- Hepatitis C kaise phailta hai? Purani syringe, ganday auzaar aur bina check kiye khoon se.
- Kya hepatitis C ka ilaaj hai? Haan, aksar log poore ilaaj se theek ho jate hain.
- Hepatitis ka test kaun sa hai? Hepatitis B ke liye HBsAg, C ke liye anti-HCV aur PCR.
- Kis doctor ko dikhayein? Gastroenterologist (liver aur maeday ka doctor).
