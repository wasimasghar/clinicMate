# Malaria

## What is malaria
Malaria is an infection caused by a parasite spread by the bite of the female Anopheles mosquito, which bites mostly at night. In Pakistan, malaria is more common after the monsoon and after floods, especially in Sindh, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

## Symptoms
Symptoms usually start 10 to 15 days after the bite:
- Fever that may come and go, with shivering and chills.
- Sweating when the fever breaks.
- Headache, body aches and tiredness.
- Nausea and vomiting.

## Severe malaria warning signs
Go to hospital at once for confusion, drowsiness, fits (seizures), difficulty breathing, dark or very little urine, yellow eyes, severe weakness, or bleeding. Children and pregnant women are at higher risk.

## Tests
Malaria is confirmed with a blood test (rapid test or blood smear). Get tested before treatment, because many fevers look like malaria.

## Treatment and care
Malaria is curable with the right treatment prescribed by a doctor. Complete the full course. Rest and drink plenty of fluids.

## Prevention
- Sleep under a mosquito net, preferably treated with insecticide.
- Use mosquito repellent and coils in the evening and at night.
- Wear long sleeves after sunset.
- Remove standing water around the house.
- If travelling to a high-risk area, ask a doctor about prevention.
