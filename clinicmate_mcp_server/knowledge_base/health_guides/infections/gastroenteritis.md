# Gastroenteritis (Diarrhoea and Vomiting)

## What is gastroenteritis
Gastroenteritis, often called "gastro" or stomach infection, is an infection of the stomach and intestines caused by viruses, bacteria or parasites from dirty water or food. It is very common in Pakistan in summer and during the monsoon.

## Symptoms
- Loose motions (diarrhoea), several times a day.
- Vomiting and nausea.
- Stomach cramps.
- Fever and weakness.
Most people get better in 2 to 3 days.

## Dehydration: the main danger
The body loses water and salts. Signs of dehydration:
- Very thirsty, dry mouth and lips.
- Little or dark urine.
- Sunken eyes, tiredness, dizziness.
- In babies: no tears, dry nappies for many hours, sunken soft spot on the head, very sleepy.

## Care at home
- Drink ORS (oral rehydration solution) after every loose motion. Make it exactly as written on the packet with clean water.
- Keep drinking water, soups, lassi and coconut water.
- Continue breastfeeding babies, and give normal soft food (khichri, yogurt, banana) as soon as the person can eat.
- Avoid sugary soft drinks and very oily food.
- Do not take any medicine to stop diarrhoea without a doctor's advice, especially in children.

## Prevention
- Drink boiled or filtered water.
- Wash hands with soap after the toilet, before eating and before cooking.
- Eat freshly cooked food. Avoid cut fruit and street food from unclean places.
- Rotavirus vaccine for babies is part of Pakistan's routine immunisation.

## When to see a doctor
- Diarrhoea for more than 2 days in adults, or more than 1 day in a baby.
- Blood in stool, high fever, or severe stomach pain.
- Vomiting so much that you cannot keep fluids down.
A General Physician or Gastroenterologist can help; take children to a Pediatrician.

## Emergency signs
Go to hospital at once for signs of severe dehydration: no urine for 8 hours or more, very drowsy or confused, cold hands and feet, or a very weak, floppy baby.
