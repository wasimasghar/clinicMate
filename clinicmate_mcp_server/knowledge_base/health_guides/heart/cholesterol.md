# High Cholesterol

## What is cholesterol
Cholesterol is a fatty substance in the blood. The body needs some, but too much "bad" cholesterol (LDL) builds up in the walls of arteries and narrows them. This increases the risk of heart attack and stroke. "Good" cholesterol (HDL) helps remove bad cholesterol.

## Symptoms
High cholesterol has no symptoms. The only way to know is a blood test.

## Test
A lipid profile blood test, usually after fasting for 9 to 12 hours, shows total cholesterol, LDL, HDL and triglycerides. Adults should get tested from about age 30 to 40, and earlier if there is a family history of heart disease, diabetes or high blood pressure.

## Causes
- Food high in saturated fat: ghee, butter, fried food, fatty meat, bakery items.
- Being overweight and inactive.
- Smoking.
- Diabetes and thyroid problems.
- Family history (some families have very high cholesterol from a young age).

## Lowering cholesterol
- Cook with less ghee and oil; choose healthier oils in small amounts.
- Eat more vegetables, fruit, lentils, beans, oats and whole grains.
- Eat fish and choose lean meat; limit red meat, organ meat and fried snacks.
- Walk or exercise at least 30 minutes most days.
- Stop smoking and keep a healthy weight.
Some people also need treatment prescribed by a doctor.

## When to see a doctor
See a Cardiologist or General Physician if your lipid profile is high, if you have diabetes or high blood pressure, or if someone in the family had a heart attack at a young age.
