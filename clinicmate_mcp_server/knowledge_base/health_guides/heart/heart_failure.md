# Heart Failure

## What is heart failure
Heart failure means the heart is not pumping blood as well as it should. It does not mean the heart has stopped. It is usually a long-term condition that can be managed with treatment and lifestyle changes.

## Causes
- Previous heart attack or blocked heart arteries.
- Long-standing high blood pressure.
- Diabetes.
- Heart valve disease, including damage from rheumatic fever in childhood.
- Irregular heartbeat and diseases of the heart muscle.

## Symptoms
- Breathlessness when walking, climbing stairs or lying flat.
- Waking up at night breathless, needing extra pillows to sleep.
- Swollen feet, ankles and legs.
- Tiredness and weakness.
- Fast weight gain from fluid (more than 2 kg in 3 days).
- Cough, especially at night.

## Living with heart failure
- Take treatment exactly as prescribed and keep follow-up visits.
- Reduce salt and follow the fluid advice from your doctor.
- Weigh yourself every morning and tell your doctor about sudden gain.
- Stay active with gentle walking as advised.
- Stop smoking and avoid tobacco.

## When to see a doctor
See a Cardiologist for breathlessness, swollen legs or tiredness that is getting worse.

## Emergency signs
Call 1122 at once for severe breathlessness at rest, chest pain, fainting, coughing pink frothy mucus, or confusion.
