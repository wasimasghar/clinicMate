# Liver Cancer

## What is liver cancer
Liver cancer is cancer that starts in the liver. In Pakistan, the biggest cause is long-standing hepatitis B or C infection, which damages the liver over many years.

## Causes and risk factors
- Chronic hepatitis B or C.
- Cirrhosis (scarring of the liver) from any cause.
- Fatty liver disease linked to obesity and diabetes.
- Heavy alcohol use.
- Food contaminated with aflatoxin (mould on poorly stored grains and nuts).

## Warning signs
Early liver cancer often has no symptoms. Later signs:
- Pain or a lump in the upper right side of the stomach.
- Weight loss and loss of appetite.
- Yellow eyes and skin (jaundice).
- Swelling of the stomach.
- Tiredness and weakness.

## Screening for people at risk
People with hepatitis B, hepatitis C or cirrhosis should get regular check-ups, usually an ultrasound and a blood test (AFP) every 6 months, as advised by their doctor. This finds cancer early when treatment works best.

## Prevention
- Get tested for hepatitis B and C, and get treated if positive.
- Get the hepatitis B vaccine.
- Avoid reused syringes and unsafe blood.
- Keep a healthy weight and control diabetes.
- Store grains and nuts in dry places and throw away mouldy food.

## When to see a doctor
See a Gastroenterologist or Oncologist if you have hepatitis or cirrhosis, or any of the warning signs.
