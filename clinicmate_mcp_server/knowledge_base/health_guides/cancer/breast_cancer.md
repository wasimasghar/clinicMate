# Breast Cancer

## What is breast cancer
Breast cancer is the growth of abnormal cells in the breast. It is the most common cancer in women in Pakistan, and Pakistan has one of the highest rates in Asia. Found early, it is very often curable. Men can also get breast cancer, though rarely.

## Warning signs
- A new lump or thickening in the breast or armpit, usually painless.
- Change in the size or shape of the breast.
- Dimpling or puckering of the skin, like orange peel.
- Nipple pulled inwards, or discharge (especially blood) from the nipple.
- Redness, rash or scaling of the nipple or breast skin.
Most lumps are not cancer, but every new lump should be checked by a doctor.

## Risk factors
- Increasing age, especially after 40.
- Family history of breast or ovarian cancer.
- Starting periods early or late menopause.
- Not breastfeeding, first child after 30.
- Being overweight and inactive.

## Early detection
- Breast self-examination once a month, a few days after the period, in front of a mirror and while lying down.
- Clinical breast examination by a doctor.
- Mammography (a breast X-ray), usually advised every 1 to 2 years from about age 40, or earlier with a strong family history. Ultrasound is often used for younger women.
Do not let fear or shyness delay a check-up. Female doctors are available.

## Treatment
Treatment depends on the stage and may include surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and other therapies, decided by an Oncologist and surgeon.

## When to see a doctor
See a Gynecologist, breast surgeon or Oncologist for any breast lump or the signs above.

## Common questions
- Chhati mein gilti (lump) mehsoos hui? Doctor ko jald dikhayein, zyada tar gilti cancer nahi hoti.
- Mammography kab karwayein? Aam taur par 40 saal ke baad.
- Kya breast cancer ka ilaaj hai? Haan, jaldi pata chale to aksar theek ho jata hai.
- Khud kaise check karun? Har mahine period ke kuch din baad aaine ke saamne aur lait kar.
