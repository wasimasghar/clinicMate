# Oral (Mouth) Cancer

## What is oral cancer
Oral cancer is cancer of the lips, tongue, cheeks, gums or floor of the mouth. It is one of the most common cancers in Pakistan, especially among men, and is strongly linked to chewing tobacco and areca nut.

## Main causes
- Gutka, mainpuri, paan with tobacco, chaliya (areca nut, betel nut).
- Naswar.
- Smoking cigarettes and shisha.
- Heavy alcohol use.
- Poor oral hygiene and sharp broken teeth rubbing the cheek.

## Warning signs
- A mouth ulcer or sore that does not heal in 2 to 3 weeks.
- White or red patches inside the mouth.
- Difficulty opening the mouth fully (stiff cheeks), often from chewing areca nut.
- A lump in the mouth or neck.
- Bleeding, numbness or pain in the mouth.
- Difficulty chewing or swallowing, loose teeth without reason.

## Prevention
- Stop gutka, paan, chaliya, naswar and smoking. It is never too late to quit.
- Clean teeth twice a day and get regular dental check-ups.
- Look inside your mouth in the mirror every month.

## When to see a doctor
See a Dentist, ENT Specialist or Oncologist for any sore, patch or lump in the mouth lasting more than 2 to 3 weeks. Early oral cancer is much easier to treat.

## Common questions
- Munh mein chhala theek nahi ho raha? 2 se 3 hafte se zyada ho to doctor ko dikhayein.
- Gutka aur chaliya se cancer hota hai? Haan, ye munh ke cancer ki sab se badi wajah hain.
- Munh poora nahi khulta? Ye chaliya ki wajah se ho sakta hai, doctor ko dikhayein.
