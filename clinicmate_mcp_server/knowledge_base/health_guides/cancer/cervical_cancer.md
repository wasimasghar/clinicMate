# Cervical Cancer

## What is cervical cancer
Cervical cancer starts in the cervix, the lower part of the womb (uterus). Almost all cases are caused by long-lasting infection with human papillomavirus (HPV). It can be prevented by vaccination and screening, and is curable when found early.

## Warning signs
Early cervical cancer usually has no symptoms. Later signs:
- Bleeding between periods, after intercourse or after menopause.
- Unusual or bad-smelling vaginal discharge.
- Pain in the lower stomach or during intercourse.

## Screening
- Pap smear or HPV test: a quick test that finds changes before they become cancer.
- Married women are usually advised to start screening around age 25 to 30 and repeat every 3 to 5 years, as advised by a Gynecologist.

## Prevention
- HPV vaccine for girls, ideally between 9 and 14 years.
- Regular screening.
- Not smoking.

## When to see a doctor
See a Gynecologist for any abnormal bleeding or discharge, or for screening. An Oncologist treats confirmed cancer.

## Common questions
- Period ke beech mein khoon aana? Gynecologist ko dikhayein.
- Pap smear kya hai? Bachedani ke munh ka chhota sa test jo cancer se pehle tabdeeli pakad leta hai.
- HPV vaccine kis umar mein? Larkiyon ke liye 9 se 14 saal behtareen hai.
