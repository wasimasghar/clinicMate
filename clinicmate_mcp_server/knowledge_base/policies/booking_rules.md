# Booking Rules

## Who can book
Any registered patient can book. To book, log in with your Pakistani mobile number. If your number is not registered, the assistant will ask your name and city to create an account.

## How booking works
1. Find a doctor by specialty, city or area.
2. Choose a free time slot.
3. The assistant shows a summary: doctor, clinic, address, day, time and fee.
4. Your appointment is booked only after you say yes.

## How far ahead you can book
Free slots are shown for the next 14 days. Each slot is 15 to 30 minutes depending on the doctor.

## One slot, one patient
A time slot can be booked by only one patient. If someone books it first, the assistant will ask you to choose another time.

## Rescheduling
You can move an upcoming appointment to another free time of the same doctor, any time before it starts. To see a different doctor, cancel and book a new appointment.

## Reviews
After your visit you can rate the doctor from 1 to 5 and write a comment. One review per appointment. Reviews are shown with your first name and the first letter of your last name.

## Cities
ClinicMate is available in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Faisalabad.

## Common questions
- Kitne din pehle appointment le sakta hoon? Agle 14 din tak.
- Kya doosre doctor par shift ho sakti hai? Nahi, cancel karke nayi booking karein.
- Kya Multan mein service hai? Abhi nahi, sirf 5 shehron mein.
