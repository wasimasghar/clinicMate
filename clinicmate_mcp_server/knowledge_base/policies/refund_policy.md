# Refund Policy

## No advance payment, no refund needed
ClinicMate does not take any payment, so there is nothing to refund when you cancel. You pay the doctor only at the clinic.

## Fee paid at the clinic
If you paid the fee at the clinic and have a problem (for example the doctor was not available after you paid), please contact the clinic directly. Clinic phone numbers are shown in the doctor's details.

## If the doctor cancels
If a doctor cannot attend, the clinic will contact you and you can book another time free of charge.

## Common questions
- Paise wapas kaise milenge? ClinicMate koi paise nahi leta. Clinic par di gayi fee ke liye clinic se rabta karein.
- Doctor nahi aaya to? Clinic se rabta karein, aur nayi appointment muft book karein.
