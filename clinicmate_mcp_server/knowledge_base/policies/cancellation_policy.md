# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel
You can cancel an upcoming appointment yourself in the ClinicMate chat or app. Ask the assistant "cancel my appointment" and confirm which one. You must be logged in with your phone number.

## Until when you can cancel
You can cancel any time before the appointment start time. After the start time, the appointment cannot be cancelled in the app.
Please cancel at least 3 hours before, so another patient can use the time.

## What happens after cancelling
The time slot becomes free again and other patients can book it. Your cancelled appointment stays in your history with the status "cancelled".

## Charges
ClinicMate does not charge any cancellation fee. You pay the doctor's fee at the clinic only when you visit.

## Common questions
- Appointment cancel kaise karun? Chat mein likhein "meri appointment cancel kar do".
- Kya cancel karne ke paise lagte hain? Nahi, koi charges nahi.
- Kya appointment ke waqt ke baad cancel ho sakti hai? Nahi, sirf shuru hone se pehle.
