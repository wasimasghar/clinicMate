# Privacy Policy

## What we store
Your name, mobile number, city, and optionally email, gender and date of birth. Your appointments, the reason you wrote for a visit, and your reviews.

## Why we store it
To book and manage your appointments, send reminders, and show your appointment history.

## Who can see it
Your appointment details are shared only with the clinic and doctor you booked. Other patients cannot see your data. Reviews show only your first name and the first letter of your last name.

## Health chat
The assistant gives general health information only. It does not diagnose and does not prescribe medicines. Do not share passwords, bank or card details in the chat.

## Your phone number
Your phone number is your login. It cannot be changed in the chat for your safety.

## Common questions
- Meri maloomat kaun dekh sakta hai? Sirf woh clinic aur doctor jahan appointment hai.
- Kya mera review mere poore naam se dikhega? Nahi, sirf pehla naam aur last name ka pehla harf.
