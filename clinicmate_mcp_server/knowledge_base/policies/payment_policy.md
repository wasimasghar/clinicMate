# Payment Policy

## Booking is free
Booking an appointment through ClinicMate is free. There is no booking fee and no advance payment.

## Paying the doctor
You pay the consultation fee directly at the clinic on the day of your visit. The fee shown in the app is the doctor's consultation fee in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

## Payment methods at the clinic
Most clinics accept cash. Many also accept debit or credit cards, JazzCash and Easypaisa. Please ask the clinic if you want to pay by card or mobile wallet.

## Extra charges
Tests, procedures, medicines and follow-up visits may cost extra and are decided by the doctor and the clinic, not by ClinicMate.

## Common questions
- Kya online payment karni hogi? Nahi, fee clinic par di jati hai.
- Booking ke paise lagte hain? Nahi, booking muft hai.
- JazzCash ya Easypaisa chalta hai? Aksar clinics mein, clinic se pooch lein.
